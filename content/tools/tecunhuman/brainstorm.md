Brainstorm is a command-line MIDI recorder from [Div's MIDI Utilities](https://github.com/dgslomin/divs-midi-utilities). It listens to incoming MIDI and automatically captures what you play, so musical ideas can be saved without starting a manual recording session.

For a practical workflow using Brainstorm with JACK and LinuxSampler, see [How I Recorded The First Brainstorm](/articles/tecunhuman/how-i-recorded-the-first-brainstorm/).
