indiegamedev.org has been updated with a broader set of TecunHuman projects and resources.

The TecunHuman [games](/games/tecunhuman/) section now includes a larger collection of open-source games, including Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS, Windows, Godot, and Unity projects. Several shared repositories were split into individual game entries so each project can be browsed on its own.

The [tools](/tools/tecunhuman/) section now includes open-source Godot audio projects such as godot-csound, godot-lv2-host, godot-vst3-host, and godot-distrho. A new [videos](/videos/tecunhuman/) section has also been added for talks, demos, and gameplay videos related to TecunHuman projects.

Developer and studio pages were updated as well, making it easier to browse TecunHuman's games, tools, videos, and related open-source work from one place.
