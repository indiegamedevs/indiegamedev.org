Werner Mendizabal is a game developer, software engineer, artist, and musician working through [TecunHuman](/studios/tecunhuman/). His projects span retro homebrew games, game jam releases, open-source fighting game work, and audio-focused tooling for Godot.

He has created a collection of open-source [games](/games/tecunhuman/), including Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS, Windows, Godot, and Unity projects. He also contributes to and maintains [indiegamedev.org](/), an open-source archive for indie game developers.
