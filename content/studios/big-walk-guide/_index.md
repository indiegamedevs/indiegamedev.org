Big Walk Guide is an independent, unofficial fan resource maintained by [szj2ys](/devs/szj2ys/). It provides English and Simplified Chinese reference pages and browser tools for co-op planning.

This is a fan-reference project, not a game-development studio. This profile uses the site's Studios taxonomy only to group the project's article contributions. It is not affiliated with House House or Panic. Its contribution here concerns the design of a small communication code, rather than the development of Big Walk itself.

Project website: [Big Walk Guide](https://thebigwalk.world/en).
