---
title: SFXMint
---

**SFXMint** is a small team that builds a library of sound effects for games, apps and videos, together with open-source tools that bring the library into game engines.

Every sound in the library is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, so it can be used in commercial games without attribution. The sounds are AI-generated or procedurally synthesized by SFXMint rather than recorded.

* [SFXMint](https://sfxmint.com) — Sound effects library and API documentation.
* [Github](https://github.com/sfxmint) — Open-source Godot plugin and other tools.
