**TecunHuman** is an independent game studio focused on creating games, developer tools, and open-source technology for game development.

The studio explores ways to make advanced creative and technical workflows more accessible to independent developers, with a particular focus on interactive audio, music technology, and the Godot Engine.

Alongside original games, TecunHuman develops open-source projects including tools for real-time audio synthesis, hosting audio plugins in Godot, and building audio plugins with Godot-based interfaces.
